(EN)Hey! I’m Laura (22). Graphic designer focused on branding and motion graphics.
Design has always been how I make sense of the world. As a kid I drew to say what I could not express. Now I do that through type, color and motion. I am inspired by human culture and art history. (ES) ¡Hola! Soy Laura (22). Diseñadora gráfica enfocada en branding y motion graphics. El diseño ha sido siempre mi forma de entender el mundo.
